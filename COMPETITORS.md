# Groundswell — Competitor Scan (2026-05-29)

Three camps. **None occupies Groundswell's exact niche:** a market-level, demand-side rent-growth *direction* call built from leading indicators (WARN/layoffs, job postings, permits), back-validated against rent indices, packaged as IC-defensible "receipts."

---

## ⭐ Closest competitor — Markerr (now part of REBA)

**Markerr** (markerr.com) — a real-estate **rent-forecasting data product**. Investors: RET Ventures, Bridge Investment Group, Pretium (user recalls a ~$5M Series A; exact round not independently confirmed). **Acquired by REBA (Real Estate Business Analytics), Dec 2025**, folding into an operational analytics platform for the rental-housing industry.

- **What they do:** Forecast rent at market AND property level. Products: *REBA Market Explorer*, *RealRent Comps*, *RealRent Forecast*, *Datasets*, + an "AI Data Scientist" that forecasts multifamily rent and explains its rationale.
- **Moat:** proprietary **data depth** — "largest source of public rent data" (MF + SFR), aggregated **income/payroll data**, 150M+ properties (title/tax), blended with job growth, income growth, population, demographics.

| Dimension | Markerr | Groundswell |
|---|---|---|
| Output | A rent **forecast number** you adopt | Direction call + **defensible range + "vs. what's priced in"** to defend/revise your own number |
| Use of labor | **Realized** job/income growth (coincident/lagging) | **Leading** indicators (WARN, fresh postings) with explicit **lead-lag backtest** |
| Moat | Proprietary data depth | **Methodology + transparency** on public data (ZORI/FRED/WARN/permits) |
| Originality | Forecasting engine | **Signal-discovery leaderboard** (which signals lead, and by how long) |
| Granularity | Property + market | **Market-level only** |
| Buyer moment | Operators/acquisitions; post-REBA drifting to **operations** | The **IC-defense / capital-allocation** moment |

**Overlap (be honest):** both forecast metro rent, both use labor inputs, both emphasize explainability. **Differentiation:** *leading* (not coincident) signal, lead-lag "receipts" framing, signal-discovery leaderboard. We can't out-data Markerr — we compete on **early-signal freshness + IC-defensibility**. Their absorption into REBA's *operations* platform pulls them away from the underwriting/IC moment — an opening.

## Two strategic findings

**1. The white space is real.** No product packages WARN/job-posting leading indicators into a metro rent-*direction* call for underwriters. The inputs (Revelio/Lightcast) and outputs (Parcl Labs/Yardi/CoStar) exist in separate silos; nobody bridges them with a defensibility layer. Incumbents compete on data depth and lag *after* demand inflects.

**2. The thesis is validated AND under pressure (important).** The labor→rent link is well-established academically (NMHC Dec-2025 jobs-vs-rent modeling; arXiv "Sticky Homelessness" 2026 uses predicted employment × supply-elasticity as an instrument for rent). BUT multiple late-2025 industry notes (NMHC, CRE Daily, Multifamily Dive) report the link is **weakening** — Q3 2025 job growth fell ~53% YoY yet rents stayed flat because **new supply now dominates**. Owners have largely shrugged off tech layoffs.

> **Implication for the build:** weight **building permits / supply at least co-equal** with the labor signals — don't ship a labor-only model. Groundswell's most *differentiated* input is **WARN's real-time, metro-granular layoff feed**, which is genuinely underexploited. The honest framing ("labor signal consistent with X%, but supply is the swing variable") is itself the defensibility edge.
